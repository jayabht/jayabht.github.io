2025 — present

Teaching a language model
what good looks like.

The brief

Alexa, Ring and Nova all generate language. Someone has to define what a good answer is before a model can be judged against it — and “sounds fine” is not a standard.

The move

Turn editorial judgement into something inspectable. Rubrics, prompt frameworks and quality guidelines that a Language Engineer, a PM and a reviewer in another timezone all read the same way.

Why it matters

It’s the same skill as a brand guideline, applied to a system that has no taste and infinite output. Consistency stops being a style question and becomes infrastructure.

The evaluation frame

01

Reasoning quality

Does the chain hold up, or does it only look like thinking?

02

Verifiable against ground truth, not against plausibility.

03

Instruction adherence

Did it do the thing asked, or an adjacent easier thing?

04

Customer relevance

Right answer, wrong person, still a failure.

05

Response consistency

The same question twice should not get two characters.

Deltic

  • Positioning
  • Campaign
  • Film

EV brand

Selling the bike is easy.
Selling momentum is the brief.

Communication route

#ChargeAheadWithDeltic

It captures the essence of what electric vehicles represent: progress, innovation, forward momentum, and sustainability. At the same time, it appeals to the daily lives of consumers who want to be ahead of the curve and embrace the latest trends and technologies.

Who we’re actually talking to

Who are we talking to when we say “charge ahead”?

It can be applied to a variety of situations, from personal growth and development to professional success and achievement. People who embody this spirit are often seen as go-getters, innovators, and leaders. They are the ones who are willing to take risks, push boundaries, and explore new horizons. By aligning ourselves with this mentality, we can position Deltic as a brand that is not just about selling electric vehicles but also about empowering people to charge ahead in their lives.

Moreover, the hashtag's relevance to daily life is clear: electric vehicles are no longer a niche product for early adopters. By using the "#ChargeAheadWithDeltic" hashtag, the brand is positioning itself as the top choice for consumers who want to be part of this transition and reap the rewards of driving an electric vehicle.

— Approach

Three things I’d
argue about.

01

Most content problems are decision problems.

By the time everyone's arguing about a word, someone upstream failed to decide who it's for. I'd rather fix it there — it's cheaper and it works.

02

A brief is a hypothesis.

If nothing could prove it wrong, it isn't a strategy. It's a preference with a deck around it.

03

AI raised the floor, not the ceiling.

Anything can produce competent copy now. What's scarce is knowing which competent thing is the right one — and being able to say why.

— About

I studied English Literature, which taught me to read very closely, and then spent five years in marketing learning that almost nobody else does. Both turned out to be useful.

The part of the job I actually like is the bit most people skip: the twenty minutes before anything gets written, working out who's on the other end and what would genuinely make them stop.

At Bewakoof I wrote for someone half-distracted, mid-scroll, looking at a lock screen at nine at night. At Amazon I write standards for a model that reads everything and understands none of it. The discipline is the same — be precise, or don't say it.
