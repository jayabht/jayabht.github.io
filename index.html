Deltic

Brand Positioning — EV

  • Brand Strategy
  • Film
  • Positioning

Selling a bike is easy. Selling momentum is the brief.

Positioning an EV challenger around the people who move first — route, campaign and brand film.

Communication Route

#ChargeAheadWithDeltic

It captures the essence of what electric vehicles represent: progress, innovation, forward momentum, and sustainability. At the same time, it appeals to the daily lives of consumers who want to be ahead of the curve and embrace the latest trends and technologies.

Read the full strategy

Who are we talking to when we say “charge ahead”?

It can be applied to a variety of situations, from personal growth and development to professional success and achievement. People who embody this spirit are often seen as go-getters, innovators, and leaders. They are the ones who are willing to take risks, push boundaries, and explore new horizons. By aligning ourselves with this mentality, we can position Deltic as a brand that is not just about selling electric vehicles but also about empowering people to charge ahead in their lives.

Moreover, the hashtag's relevance to daily life is clear: electric vehicles are no longer a niche product for early adopters. By using the "#ChargeAheadWithDeltic" hashtag, the brand is positioning itself as the top choice for consumers who want to be part of this transition and reap the rewards of driving an electric vehicle.

Brand Film — full script

Insight: It’s a known belief that children learn from their parents. Therefore, it’s the parent’s responsibility to set the ideal example for their kids.

The screen opens with a kid learning about “change” in his online class.

The teacher shares with the students a famous quote from Gandhiji.

The screen zooms out & the background faded. His father comes to focus, talking on his phone.

Father: Sir, jo steps liya hai usse change hojaega pattern.

Boss (distant voice from phone): Itna confidently keh rahe ho, test ke time mein it didn’t work the way you imagined.

Father: Sir, meri baat toh suniye…

<call gets disconnected>

Cut to a shot of the father going out of the house in an urgent manner.

The kid follows him with inquisitiveness in his eyes & asks if he can join him. Dad shrugs & says “Hop on”

<Show montages of the dad going to a far-off beach to clean the surroundings while the kid observes him intently>

<Shots of him driving to his mother’s house to feed her medicine while the kid watches him>

<Finally, he drives to his office to fix the coding issue while the kid waits while sipping juice.>

Dad: Hey, buddy, chalein kya?

Kid <nods eagerly, he’s in full awe of his dad>

<Shot of home>

We can see the kid appearing for his public speaking assessment- Who’s the ideal changemaker?

Kid- For me, the ideal changemaker is the one who charges ahead despite the things that hold him back. I’m lucky to know someone like that, my dad!

<Cut to a shot of his dad preparing his Deltic bike again for a ride>

Outro copy:

Deltic

Ride that Powers you

And inspires the next gen.

#ChargeAheadWithDeltic
