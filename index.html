Communication Route

#ChargeAheadWithDeltic

It captures the essence of what electric vehicles represent: progress, innovation, forward momentum, and sustainability. At the same time, it appeals to the daily lives of consumers who want to be ahead of the curve and embrace the latest trends and technologies.

Read the strategy

Who are we talking to when we say “charge ahead”?

It can be applied to a variety of situations, from personal growth and development to professional success and achievement. People who embody this spirit are often seen as go-getters, innovators, and leaders. They are the ones who are willing to take risks, push boundaries, and explore new horizons. By aligning ourselves with this mentality, we can position Deltic as a brand that is not just about selling electric vehicles but also about empowering people to charge ahead in their lives.

Moreover, the hashtag's relevance to daily life is clear: electric vehicles are no longer a niche product for early adopters. By using the "#ChargeAheadWithDeltic" hashtag, the brand is positioning itself as the top choice for consumers who want to be part of this transition and reap the rewards of driving an electric vehicle.
